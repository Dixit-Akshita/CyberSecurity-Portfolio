🛡️ Cyber Security Awareness Dashboard

🔍 Phishing Attacks

Fake websites/emails used to steal sensitive data.

  • Check URL carefully
  • Avoid unknown links
  • Never enter credentials blindly

💰 UPI Frauds

  • PIN is for sending money only
  • Don’t scan unknown QR codes
  • Ignore unknown requests

📈 Investment Scams

  • No guaranteed profits
  • Verify platforms
  • Avoid WhatsApp trading groups

📞 Fake Calls

  • No real officer asks money
  • Don’t panic
  • Verify identity

💼 Job Scams

  • No job requires payment
  • Check company details
  • Avoid fake offers

🧠 AI Scams

  • Beware of deepfake calls
  • Verify before trusting
  • Don’t share sensitive info

🌐 Safe Practices

  • Use strong passwords
  • Enable 2FA
  • Update software
  • Avoid public WiFi

📰 Latest Trends

  • UPI frauds rising
  • Fake investment apps
  • AI phishing attacks
  • Job & loan scams
