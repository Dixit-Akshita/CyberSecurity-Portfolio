# Overview

The Brochure serves as the warm-up challenge for Hacker Holidays 2026.

Rather than exploiting a target, the objective is to investigate publicly available information and discover details hidden in plain sight.

This room introduces the fundamentals of Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) through a realistic scenario involving the fictional Byte Lotus Hotel.

---

# Objectives

- Practice OSINT techniques
- Observe digital footprints
- Analyze publicly available information
- Develop an investigative mindset

---

# Skills Practiced

- Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)
- Digital Footprinting
- Social Media Investigation
- Information Correlation
- Critical Observation
- Basic Data Analysis

---

# Investigation Summary

- The investigation begins with promotional material for the Byte Lotus Hotel.

- Instead of accepting the information at face value, the challenge encourages examining available content more closely to identify inconsistencies and hidden clues.

- By following publicly accessible information, it becomes possible to connect pieces of evidence and gradually uncover information that isn't immediately obvious.

- The room emphasizes thinking like an investigator rather than relying on technical exploitation.

---

# Tools Used

- Web Browser
- Search Engines
- Social Media Platforms
- CyberChef (for decoding)
- Publicly Available Information Sources

---

# Key Takeaways

- Valuable information is often publicly accessible.
- Small clues can reveal larger patterns when connected.
- OSINT relies more on observation than technical exploitation.
- Social media can unintentionally expose useful intelligence.
- Always verify information from multiple sources before drawing conclusions.

---

# What I Learned

- This room demonstrated that cybersecurity investigations are not always about hacking systems.

- Collecting, analyzing, and correlating publicly available information can provide valuable intelligence without interacting directly with a target.

- It also reinforced the importance of maintaining operational security (OPSEC) while conducting investigations.

# Reflection

### Biggest Takeaway

Developing an investigative mindset is just as important as learning technical tools.

### Biggest Challenge

Identifying meaningful clues among publicly available information without making assumptions.
