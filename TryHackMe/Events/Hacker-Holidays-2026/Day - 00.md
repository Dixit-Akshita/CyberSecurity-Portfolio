# Day 0 – The Brochure

## Overview

The introductory room of the **TryHackMe Hacker Holidays 2026 CTF** focused on using **Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)** to gather publicly available information. The challenge demonstrated how seemingly harmless online content can reveal valuable clues during an investigation.

## Room Details

* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Event:** Hacker Holidays 2026
* **Room:** The Brochure
* **Category:** OSINT
* **Difficulty:** Very Easy

## Scenario

Before arriving at the Byte Lotus Hotel, participants investigated its online presence. A promotional brochure contained subtle clues that encouraged deeper exploration beyond what was immediately visible, highlighting the importance of digital footprint analysis.

## Objectives

* Analyze publicly available information.
* Investigate digital assets related to the scenario.
* Identify hidden clues using OSINT techniques.

## Concepts Covered

* Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)
* Digital Footprint Analysis
* Reverse Image Analysis
* Social Media Investigation
* Metadata Awareness

## What I Learned

* Valuable intelligence can often be gathered from publicly available sources.
* Images and social media posts may reveal more information than intended.
* Effective investigations begin with structured reconnaissance and observation.
* Small details can provide meaningful leads during an investigation.

## Key Takeaways

* OSINT is a foundational skill for cybersecurity investigations.
* Information collected from multiple public sources can be correlated to build a clearer picture.
* Developing an investigator's mindset is just as important as knowing the available tools.
* Ethical collection and analysis of public information is essential in cybersecurity.

## Skills Practiced

* Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)
* Digital Reconnaissance
* Information Gathering
* Critical Thinking
* Analytical Investigation
