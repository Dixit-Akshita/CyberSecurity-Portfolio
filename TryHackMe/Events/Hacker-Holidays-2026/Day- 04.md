# Day 4 – Packed Light

## Overview

The fourth room of the **TryHackMe Hacker Holidays 2026 CTF** focused on **Network Forensics**. The challenge involved analyzing captured network traffic to identify suspicious activity, reconstruct hidden data, and understand how information can be concealed within seemingly normal communications.

## Room Details

* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Event:** Hacker Holidays 2026
* **Room:** Packed Light
* **Category:** Forensics
* **Difficulty:** Easy

## Scenario

A network capture from the Byte Lotus Hotel guest network revealed unusual traffic occurring at regular intervals. The challenge required investigating the captured packets, identifying suspicious patterns, and determining whether data was being transferred through a covert communication channel.

## Objectives

* Analyze the provided packet capture.
* Identify suspicious communication patterns.
* Investigate potential covert channels.
* Recover and analyze hidden data.
* Complete the challenge objectives.

## Concepts Covered

* Network Forensics
* PCAP Analysis
* Network Traffic Investigation
* Covert Communication Channels
* Cryptography

## What I Learned

* Packet captures provide valuable evidence during forensic investigations.
* Repetitive or unusual network behavior can indicate suspicious activity.
* Hidden information can be embedded within seemingly legitimate network communications.
* Combining traffic analysis and decoding techniques helps uncover concealed data.

## Key Takeaways

* Network forensics requires both technical analysis and investigative thinking.
* Small anomalies in network traffic can reveal larger security concerns.
* PCAP analysis is a fundamental skill for incident response and digital forensics.
* Understanding communication patterns is as important as examining packet contents.
