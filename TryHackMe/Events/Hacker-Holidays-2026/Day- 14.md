# Day 14 – Management Wants a Word

## Overview

The final room of the **TryHackMe Hacker Holidays 2026 CTF** focused on **Windows Forensics** and required investigating a collection of artifacts from a guest's laptop.

The challenge involved correlating evidence across the system, identifying information that had been unintentionally left behind, and using the discovered information to complete the investigation.

## Room Details

* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Event:** Hacker Holidays 2026
* **Room:** Management Wants a Word
* **Category:** Forensics
* **Difficulty:** Hard

## Scenario

A guest's laptop was recovered after an early checkout and underwent forensic triage before being wiped. The investigation required examining the artifacts left behind on the system and connecting the available evidence to uncover information that had been intended to remain private.

## Objectives

* Analyze the provided forensic artifacts.
* Investigate evidence from the Windows system.
* Identify relevant information hidden within the artifacts.
* Correlate findings from multiple sources.
* Complete the investigation.

## Concepts Covered

* Windows Forensics
* Digital Artifact Analysis
* Browser Forensics
* Evidence Correlation
* Cryptography

## What I Learned

* Digital artifacts can retain valuable information long after users believe it has been removed or forgotten.
* Browser artifacts can provide important forensic evidence during investigations.
* Effective forensic analysis often requires correlating information from multiple sources.
* Cryptographic concepts can be relevant when investigating protected or encoded information.

## Key Takeaways

* A forensic investigation should consider the entire digital environment rather than focusing on a single artifact.
* Browser and system artifacts can reveal important details about user activity.
* Evidence correlation is essential for building a complete picture during an investigation.
* Proper data handling and secure information management are important for preventing unintended exposure.
