# Day 7 – Do Not Disturb

## Overview

The seventh room of the **TryHackMe Hacker Holidays 2026 CTF** focused on **Boot2Root** concepts. The challenge involved investigating a web-based environment, identifying weaknesses in access controls, and progressing through multiple stages to gain elevated access within the target system.

## Room Details

* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Event:** Hacker Holidays 2026
* **Room:** Do Not Disturb
* **Category:** Boot2Root
* **Difficulty:** Medium

## Scenario

At the Byte Lotus Hotel, unusual activity suggested that someone already had access they should not possess. The challenge required following the traces left behind, investigating the environment, and understanding how weaknesses in access management could be leveraged to gain deeper access to the system.

## Objectives

* Investigate the target environment.
* Identify security weaknesses.
* Gain access to the system.
* Escalate privileges where possible.
* Complete the challenge objectives.

## Concepts Covered

* Web Security
* Boot2Root Methodology
* Session Management
* Authentication & Authorization
* Privilege Escalation

## What I Learned

* Access control weaknesses can lead to unauthorized system access.
* Session management plays a critical role in application security.
* Security investigations often require combining multiple observations to uncover a complete attack path.
* Privilege escalation remains a key stage in many real-world attack scenarios.

## Key Takeaways

* Proper authentication and authorization controls are essential for protecting systems.
* Security misconfigurations can create unintended access opportunities.
* Understanding how users and sessions are managed helps identify potential risks.
* Layered security controls help reduce the impact of individual weaknesses.
