# Day 6 – Overheard at Breakfast

## Overview

The sixth room of the **TryHackMe Hacker Holidays 2026 CTF** focused on **Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)**. The challenge demonstrated how publicly available information, when carefully analyzed and correlated, can reveal a larger digital footprint than initially expected.

## Room Details

* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Event:** Hacker Holidays 2026
* **Room:** Overheard at Breakfast
* **Category:** OSINT
* **Difficulty:** Easy

## Scenario

A captured conversation from the Byte Lotus Hotel breakfast terrace contained subtle clues about an individual's online presence. The challenge required analyzing the available information, identifying useful indicators, and following digital breadcrumbs to uncover a hidden account.

## Objectives

* Analyze the provided conversation.
* Identify relevant clues and indicators.
* Investigate publicly available information.
* Locate the hidden account.
* Complete the challenge objectives.

## Concepts Covered

* Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)
* Social Media Investigation
* Digital Footprint Analysis
* Hashing Concepts
* Information Correlation

## What I Learned

* Small pieces of publicly available information can often be linked together to reveal additional details.
* OSINT investigations rely heavily on observation, correlation, and validation.
* Digital footprints can extend across multiple platforms and services.
* Hashing and data transformation techniques can play a role in OSINT investigations.

## Key Takeaways

* Effective OSINT is about connecting information from multiple sources.
* Publicly shared information can unintentionally reveal more than expected.
* Verification and correlation are essential during investigations.
* Investigative thinking is often more valuable than relying solely on tools.
