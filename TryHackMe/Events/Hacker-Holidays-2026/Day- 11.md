# Day 11 – Infinity Pool

## Overview

The eleventh room of the **TryHackMe Hacker Holidays 2026 CTF** focused on **Boot2Root** concepts. The challenge required investigating a target environment, identifying accessible systems, and progressing through multiple stages to gain elevated access.

## Room Details

* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Event:** Hacker Holidays 2026
* **Room:** Infinity Pool
* **Category:** Boot2Root
* **Difficulty:** Medium

## Scenario

The Byte Lotus Hotel environment contained systems that were not immediately visible to users. The challenge encouraged participants to explore the environment, identify overlooked assets, and understand how hidden systems can contribute to an organization's attack surface.

## Objectives

* Investigate the target environment.
* Identify accessible systems and services.
* Assess potential security weaknesses.
* Gain elevated access.
* Complete the challenge objectives.

## Concepts Covered

* Boot2Root
* System Enumeration
* Attack Surface Discovery
* Web Security
* Privilege Escalation

## What I Learned

* Thorough enumeration is often the most important phase of a security assessment.
* Hidden or undocumented systems can introduce additional security risks.
* Understanding how systems interact helps uncover potential attack paths.
* Privilege escalation often depends on identifying weaknesses discovered during earlier stages of an assessment.

## Key Takeaways

* Enumeration should never be rushed or overlooked.
* Every exposed system increases the overall attack surface.
* Security assessments require both technical knowledge and investigative thinking.
* Layered security controls help reduce risk when individual systems are exposed.
